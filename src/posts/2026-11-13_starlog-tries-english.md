Hello, traveler, and welcome to my blog – the starlog. Where I’m sitting right now, it is a cold and dark evening. But I closed the curtains on it. Inside it is warm and a 
calming tune is playing.

This post shall serve as my introduction to you. I wander the world wide web under the name »Misteldohr«. My main 
craft has always been drawing and I came to worldbuilding through drawing comics. Right now I am working on a solo TTRPG set in a mysterious fantasy-tinged future.

## What this place is
Here on my blog I talk about any hobby or interest that occupies my mind right now. Or it may just be thoughts and reflections too. Think of it as a logbook of whatever drifts into my orbit. What you will most likely find are posts about art. Like [that one](https://mdohr.space/posts/2026-09-28_experiment-mit-farbe/) (in German) when I tried acrylics on gouache on watercolor – I do not recommend it. But there will also be entries about TTRPGs, the occasional zine, and maybe a bicycle ride.

## Why English now?
So far, I have written this blog in my mother tongue, which is German. As an experiment I am trying to switch to English. And while Germans don't have the reputation for being funny, I swear my German self is funnier than I might be able to express in English. I'll try to sound authentic to myself. 

What I am hoping to find is like-minded people, contacts, other creators. Connection, really. And I thought the chances might be better in English, to be honest.

I don't have a comment system, but you can always reach me via email (link at the end of the post) or on one of the platforms lined up in the footer below.

What are you making right now?
